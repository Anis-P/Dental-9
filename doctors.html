Specialists You Can Trust

Every doctor at Sharma Dental Care is not only highly qualified but also deeply committed to providing care with empathy, patience, and precision.

Founder & Chief Dentist

Dr. Rahul Sharma

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon
BDS, MDS (Oral Surgery) 18 Yrs Exp.

Dr. Rahul Sharma is the founder of Sharma Dental Care and one of Mumbai's most respected oral surgeons. Trained at the prestigious KEM Hospital, Mumbai, he completed his MDS with honours and subsequently underwent advanced implantology training in South Korea and Germany. Dr. Sharma has performed over 2,000 dental implants, hundreds of complex extractions, and numerous jaw surgeries. He is known for his calm demeanour and his ability to explain complex procedures in simple terms, making patients feel completely at ease.

Cosmetic Specialist

Dr. Saurav Mehta

Orthodontist & Cosmetic Dentist
BDS, MDS (Orthodontics) 12 Yrs Exp.

Dr. Saurav Mehta is our star orthodontist and cosmetic dental specialist. After completing his MDS in Orthodontics from Nair Hospital Dental College, Mumbai, he pursued a Fellowship in Cosmetic Dentistry from the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry in the USA. With over 1,500 orthodontic cases treated and hundreds of smile makeovers delivered, Dr. Mehta is known for combining clinical precision with an artist's eye for aesthetics. He is a certified Invisalign provider and a specialist in digital smile design.

Endodontist

Dr. Amit Verma

Specialist Endodontist
BDS, MDS (Endodontics) 10 Yrs Exp.

Dr. Amit Verma is our specialist endodontist with a reputation for performing virtually painless root canal treatments. Trained at the Government Dental College & Hospital, Mumbai, Dr. Verma uses the latest rotary endodontic systems and operates under dental loupes with magnification for unmatched precision. He has successfully completed over 5,000 root canal treatments with a 99% success rate. His methodical approach and gentle technique have converted countless dental-phobic patients into loyal advocates for the clinic.

Periodontist

Dr. Sneha Joshi

Gum Disease & Periodontist
BDS, MDS (Periodontics) 9 Yrs Exp.

Dr. Sneha Joshi is our specialist in gum diseases and periodontal surgery. She completed her post-graduation in Periodontics from the prestigious Manipal College of Dental Sciences and has since become one of the most sought-after periodontists in western Mumbai. Dr. Joshi is proficient in laser gum surgery, gum grafting, and the non-surgical management of advanced periodontitis. Her patients appreciate her thorough approach to gum health and her ability to reverse early stages of gum disease with non-surgical techniques.

Prosthodontist

Dr. Vikram Nair

Crowns, Bridges & Prosthetics
BDS, MDS (Prosthodontics) 11 Yrs Exp.

Dr. Vikram Nair is our prosthodontics specialist, responsible for restoring and replacing teeth with crowns, bridges, dentures, and implant-supported restorations. A graduate from Sinhgad Dental College, Pune, Dr. Nair works with our in-house CAD/CAM technology to fabricate same-day ceramic crowns that fit with extraordinary precision. His meticulous attention to detail ensures that every crown, bridge, and veneer he creates blends seamlessly with the patient's natural teeth in both form and function.

Children's Dental Specialist

Dr. Kavita Iyer is our beloved children's dentist — a specialist who has a remarkable gift for making children feel comfortable and confident during their dental visits.
