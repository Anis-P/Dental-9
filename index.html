About Our Clinic

18 Years of Excellence in Dental Care

Sharma Dental Care was founded in 2006 by Dr. Rahul Sharma with a simple but powerful mission: to provide Mumbai residents with world-class dental treatment at honest, transparent pricing. Over the years, we have grown into one of Andheri's most trusted dental destinations.

Our clinic combines cutting-edge technology — digital X-rays, laser dentistry, CAD/CAM crowns — with a warm, patient-first approach that makes even the most anxious visitors feel at ease.

  • State-of-the-art digital X-ray & 3D imaging equipment
  • Sterilized instruments & hospital-grade hygiene protocols
  • EMI options available — No-cost EMI on major treatments
  • Evening & weekend appointments for working professionals
