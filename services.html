Preventive Care

🪥 Teeth Cleaning

Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) is the foundation of good oral health. Despite regular brushing at home, plaque and tartar accumulate in areas that a toothbrush simply cannot reach — particularly between teeth and along the gum line. Over time, this buildup leads to gum disease, tooth decay, and bad breath.

We follow with a professional polishing using a rotating brush and fine paste to remove surface stains and leave your teeth visibly brighter and smoother.

₹ 800 – ₹ 2,000 per session
Restorative Care

🔬 Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment (RCT) has an undeserved reputation for being painful — but with modern techniques and anaesthesia, it's no more uncomfortable than getting a filling.

RCT is needed when the pulp (soft tissue inside the tooth containing nerves and blood vessels) becomes infected or inflamed. Without treatment, the infection can spread and the tooth may need to be extracted.

Our endodontist Dr. Amit Verma specializes in single-sitting root canal treatments, meaning in many cases we can complete the entire procedure in one visit, saving you time and reducing discomfort.

₹ 3,500 – ₹ 9,000 per tooth (incl. crown)
Permanent Solution

🦷 Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over a period of 3–6 months in a process called osseointegration. Once integrated, a custom ceramic crown is attached — creating a tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

We use premium Swiss-made Straumann implants and Korean Osstem implants — two of the most research-backed systems available worldwide. Our implants come with a lifetime warranty on the implant fixture itself.

Orthodontic Treatment

😁 Braces & Aligners

Orthodontic treatment straightens misaligned teeth, corrects bite issues, and creates the perfectly even smile you've always dreamed of. At Sharma Dental Care, we offer every type of orthodontic option to suit your lifestyle, budget, and clinical requirements.

Our options include traditional metal braces (most effective for complex cases), tooth-coloured ceramic braces (discreet and aesthetic), lingual braces (placed behind the teeth — completely invisible from the front), and Invisalign clear aligners (removable, transparent trays that are perfect for adults and image-conscious teenagers).

Our specialist orthodontist Dr. Priya Mehta has treated over 1,500 cases and uses digital smile simulation software to show you your expected result before treatment even begins.

Cosmetic Dentistry

✨ Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is one of the simplest, fastest ways to dramatically improve your smile. Over time, teeth naturally darken due to food, beverages like tea, coffee, and red wine, tobacco use, ageing, and certain medications. Our professional treatments are far more effective and safer than over-the-counter products.

We offer in-office Zoom! Whitening — a one-hour chairside procedure using a powerful whitening gel activated by a special LED light. Most patients see teeth lighten by 6–8 shades in a single session. We also offer custom take-home whitening kits with professional-grade trays and prescription-strength gel for gradual whitening from the comfort of your home.

Benefits

₹ 6,000 – ₹ 15,000 in-office or take-home
Complete Transformation

💎 Smile Makeover

A smile makeover is a comprehensive, customized treatment plan that combines multiple dental procedures to completely transform the appearance of your smile. It's not a single treatment but a tailored combination of cosmetic and restorative procedures designed specifically for your unique facial features, skin tone, and aesthetic goals.

A typical smile makeover at Sharma Dental Care may include teeth whitening, porcelain veneers to reshape and resurface teeth, crowns to restore damaged teeth, orthodontic alignment, gum contouring, and dental implants if needed. We use cutting-edge digital smile design software to preview your result before any treatment begins.

Our smile makeover specialist Dr. Priya Mehta has transformed hundreds of smiles — from minor enhancements to complete full-mouth rehabilitations. Whether you're preparing for a wedding, a big event, or simply want to invest in yourself — a smile makeover can be life-changing.

May Include

  • Porcelain veneers — reshape, resize, recolour teeth
  • Professional whitening for uniform brightness
  • Orthodontic alignment with Invisalign or braces
  • Gum contouring for a balanced smile line
  • Ceramic crowns to restore damaged or worn teeth
  • Dental implants for any missing teeth

🎨 Digital Smile Design

We use DSD (Digital Smile Design) technology to create a digital preview of your new smile on photos of your face — so you know exactly what to expect before committing to treatment.
