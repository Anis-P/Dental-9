Our Story

A Journey Built on Trust & Care

Sharma Dental Care was founded in 2006 by Dr. Rahul Sharma, a young but passionate dentist who had just completed his Masters in Oral Surgery from KEM Hospital, Mumbai. Dr. Sharma had one dream: to create a dental clinic that would make quality oral healthcare accessible to every family in Mumbai — without compromise on standards, ethics, or warmth.

Starting with a modest two-chair setup in Andheri West, the clinic quickly earned a reputation for exceptional results and patient-centric care. Word spread through the neighbourhood, and within three years, Sharma Dental Care had expanded to a fully equipped six-chair dental facility — one of the most advanced in the western suburbs of Mumbai.

Today, with a team of 12 specialists, state-of-the-art equipment, and over 15,000 happy patients, Sharma Dental Care stands as a beacon of dental excellence in Mumbai. Our journey has been defined not by accolades — though we've won many — but by the thousands of smiles we've transformed and the trust families have placed in us year after year.

We have invested heavily in the latest dental technologies including 3D cone beam CT scanning, digital impressions (iTero scanner), same-day CAD/CAM crowns, laser gum therapy, and painless computer-controlled anaesthesia delivery. But technology alone doesn't make great dentistry — it's the human touch, the empathy, and the genuine care that sets our team apart.

As we look towards the future, our commitment remains unchanged: to serve our patients with the highest standards of clinical excellence, to treat every patient like family, and to continue earning the trust of Mumbai, one smile at a time.
